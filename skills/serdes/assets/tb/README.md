# SerDes Process-Independent Spectre X Testbench Suites

Self-contained, PDK-agnostic Spectre X (`+preset=cx` / `+preset=ax`) testbench suites (`10` suites, `35` executable `.scs` testbenches + causal `mtline` & synthetic `.s4p` channels) covering every block and end-to-end top-level link of a high-speed NRZ & PAM4 wireline transceiver.

All analyzers are zero-dependency Python (`numpy` only) backed by [`common/psf_utils.py`](../../../../common/psf_utils.py).

| Suite | Decks | Primary Verification Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| [`channel/`](channel/README.md) | `channel_diff_lossy.scs`, `channel_synth_causal.s4p`, `tb_channel_sp_tdr.scs`, `tb_channel_sbr_eye.scs`, `tb_channel_nport_s4p.scs`, `tb_channel_xtalk_fext_next.scs`, `tb_channel_blw_cid.scs`, `analyze_channel.py` | 4-port `SDD21`/`SDD11`, TDR `Z_TDR(t)`, SBR (`h_-1, h0, h1..h5` & PDA eye), `nport` `bbspice` causal `.s4p` convolution, FEXT/NEXT crosstalk eye degradation, AC-coupled CID baseline wander (`blw_peak_mv`, pre/post-CID eye) |
| [`tx_ffe/`](tx_ffe/README.md) | `tb_tx_s11.scs`, `tb_dc_termination.scs`, `tb_tx_ffe_channel.scs`, `tb_tx_cm_skew_sndr.scs`, `analyze_tx_ffe.py` | TX `SDD11(f)`, DC termination `R_term(V)` flatness, 4-tap TX FFE SBR & PRBS eye opening, 20–80% `t_r`/`t_f`, P/N skew, `V_cm,ac` & IEEE 802.3 Clause 93/120 linear-fit `SNDR_TX` |
| [`ctle_vga/`](ctle_vga/README.md) | `tb_ctle_ac_noise.scs`, `tb_ctle_channel_ac.scs`, `tb_ctle_channel_eye.scs`, `tb_ctle_linearity_p1db.scs`, `tb_vga_offset_cal.scs`, `analyze_ctle_vga.py` | Standalone AC peaking/noise, combined `H_ch(f)*H_ctle(f)` DC-to-Nyquist flatness, before/after eye opening, coherent `P1dB`/`HD3`/`SFDR`, closed-loop offset calibration |

| [`clocking_jitter/`](clocking_jitter/README.md) | `tb_clk_dcd_iq_skew.scs`, `tb_tx_jitter_bathtub.scs`, `tb_psij_supply_noise.scs`, `analyze_clocking_jitter.py` | Half-rate DCD (`%`/`ps`) & 90-degree I/Q skew (`deg`/`ps`) before vs. after cal; TX `DCD`/`DDJ`/`SJ`/`RJ` decomposition & `BER = 1e-12` bathtub eye width; clock `PSIJ [ps/mV, mUI/mV]` & RX CTLE `PSRR [dB]` |
